Experience

AI Researcher 2022 – Present
Center for Applied AI (CAAI), University of Kentucky

Leading research on self-supervised vision transformers for medical imaging across multiple projects:

  • HeartLens – Built an AI diagnostic tool for coronary artery calcium (CAC) detection and Agatston risk scoring in CT scans. Designed a three-stage pipeline: (1) self-supervised DINO ViT pre-trained on 914 CT scans; (2) linear classifier for calcified slice identification; (3) U-Net segmentation for lesion-level scoring. Applied to a UK HealthCare dataset of 3,100 cases, 191,457 CT slices, and 8,424 annotations. Led interdisciplinary collaboration with UK HealthCare Radiology and Cardiology divisions. Produced two publications: DINO-LG (89% sensitivity, 90% specificity) and CARD-ViT (0.910 accuracy on gated CT).
  • DINO-MX Framework – Designed and implemented a unified, configuration-driven training framework combining DINO, DINOv2, and DINOv3 objectives. Supports ViT-S/B/L/G architectures, LoRA fine-tuning, multi-teacher distillation, layer freezing, and DDP/FSDP distributed training with full HuggingFace compatibility.
  • Vision Foundry / Cat-Vision – Lead developer of a self-service platform for AI-powered image analysis targeting biomedical researchers with unlabeled datasets. Deployed on CAAI's NVIDIA DGX cyberinfrastructure with ClearML experiment tracking.
  • MAD-NP – Produced a neuropathology foundation model using Magnification-Aware Distillation on whole-slide images, supporting tissue segmentation and large-scale similarity search. Cancer foundational model developed for NeurIPS 2025 SLC-PFM competition.
  • GVHD Classification – Built an end-to-end ML pipeline for histopathology image classification using Prov-Gigapath foundation model features, achieving AUC 0.982 and 87.7% accuracy.
Software Developer – RAM Project 2021 – 2024
Healthier Futures Lab (HealthFuL), University of Kentucky

Developed an Android application for remote alcohol monitoring supporting contingency management therapy for alcohol use disorder. Implemented Bluetooth auto-connection to BACtrack breathalyzer with guided on-screen prompts, captured facial photographs for biometric identity verification, and transmitted readings to a HIPAA-compliant secure server. Extended with offline buffering, prize-based incentive protocol, and CO monitor integration for smoking cessation monitoring.

NeurIPS 2025 – Invited Participant 2025
SLC-PFM: Self-supervised Learning for Cancer Pathology Foundation Models

Invited to participate in developing next-generation pathology foundation models using self-supervised learning on ~300M pathology images across 39 cancer types. Models evaluated across 23 clinically relevant downstream tasks by leading cancer centers including MSK, Mayo Clinic, and MD Anderson. Abstract accepted.
